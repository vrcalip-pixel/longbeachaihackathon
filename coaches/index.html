Coach guide

You run the room and hold the clock. You do not need to know AI; the roaming mentors handle tools. Success is every squad pitching at 1:30 with something on a screen and every student saying at least one sentence.

Download the coach pack (PDF)   Scoresheets (PDF)

Your job

Do and don’t

  • You do: keep time, ask questions, make sure quiet students get a say, run the pitch-off.
  • You do not: fix tools (wave down a mentor), choose the idea, touch a keyboard, or present.
  • If something is wrong (a student unwell, upset, or missing): find any staff member in an event shirt. Do not leave the room unattended with minors in it.

Any track

Questions that work

  • Who exactly is this for? What is their name?
  • What happens today, without your idea?
  • What does the AI read, and what does it produce?
  • How would you know if the AI got it wrong?
  • Who could this hurt or leave out?
  • If you only had 30 more minutes, what would you cut?
Stuck

When a squad stalls

The idea is the whole sector (“fix healthcare”)Ask for one person and one bad day.
Arguing over two ideasTwo-minute pitch each, squad votes, move on.
One student doing everythingGive roles: builder, researcher, designer, ethics lead, pitch lead, timekeeper.
A tool will not workFlag a mentor; meanwhile the squad writes the pitch.
Nothing built by 12:45Switch to a plan with one worked example. That is a valid entry.
A student has gone silentAsk them a direct, easy question about the person the squad chose.
1:30

Running the pitch-off

  1. Draw the order from a hat at 1:25.
  2. Each squad gets 3 minutes. Hold up a hand at 2:30. Stop them at 3:00, mid-sentence if needed.
  3. Ask one question, the same for every squad: “What is the biggest risk in your idea, and what did you do about it?”
  4. You and one mentor each fill in a scoresheet without conferring. Add the two totals.
  5. Highest total advances. A tie goes to the higher responsible-design score.
  6. Announce the finalist, then ask every other squad to name one thing they are proud of. Nobody leaves having only lost.
  7. Remind the finalist: 3 minutes again in Dyer Hall at 2:15, same pitch, all six on stage.
